Topics in Combinatorial Optimization (Autumn 2026)
=================================================

Textbooks and further reading
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
There is no required textbook. Below are for further reading.

- Korte and Vygen, `Combinatorial Optimization: Theory and Algorithms <https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-662-56039-6>`_, 6th ed., Springer, 2018.  
- Schrijver, Combinatorial Optimization: Polyhedra and Efficiency, Springer, 2003. 
- Goemans, `Lecture notes of 18.453 Combinatorial Optimization <https://math.mit.edu/~goemans/18453S17/18453.html>`_, MIT, 2017. 


Topics
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

1. Intro + Polyhedral Combinatorics

   - Introduction to combinatorial optimization
   - Review of linear programming
   - Integral polyhedra and totally unimodular matrices
   
   📝Further reading: `Michel Goemans' lecture note on linear programming and polyhedral combinatorics <https://math.mit.edu/~goemans/18453S17/polyhedral.pdf>`_

2. Bipartite Matching I

   - Min-max theorems for bipartite matching
   - Unweighted bipartite matching
   - Weighted bipartite matching

3. Bipartite Matching II

   - The shortest path problem
   - The successive shortest path method and the primal-dual method
   - A perspective based on optimality conditions

4. Maximum Flow I

   - The maximum flow problem
   - Modeling with maximum flow

5. Maximum Flow II

   - The Ford-Fulkerson algorithm
   - The Edmonds-Karp algorithm

6. Minimum-Cost Flow

   - The minimum-cost flow problem
   - Algorithms

7. Matroids

   - Introduction to matroids
   - Minimum-weight independent sets and the greedy algorithm
   - Matroid polytopes

8. Independent Matching and Matroid Intersection

   - Independent matching
   - Circuits
   - Algorithms

9. Submodular Minimization

   - Examples of submodular functions
   - The Lovász extension
   - The minimum-norm point algorithm

10. Submodular Maximization

    - Problem formulation
    - Modeling examples
    - The greedy algorithm

11. Selected Topics I

12. Selected Topics II

13. Selected Topics III
